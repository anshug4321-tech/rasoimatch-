    How good are the matches?

    The data has no answer key, so the test is built from the recipes themselves. For a recipe, the app hides 30% of its ingredients, searches with the other 70%, and checks where the original recipe lands in the ranking. Every number below is computed in your browser when you press the button. Nothing is typed in by hand.

    Recipes with at least 5 core ingredients are split 70% development / 30% test, balanced by region group, seed 42. Weights are tuned on the development recipes only. The test recipes are used only for the final report.
